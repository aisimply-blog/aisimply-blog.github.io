Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 29, 2026

1. Overview

AISimply respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under applicable laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The short version: We collect virtually no personal data. Our website is designed to be private by default. You can read all our content without providing any information about yourself.

2. Data We Collect

Personal Data

We do not collect personal data. AISimply does not ask for your name, email address, location, or any other identifying information. You can visit our site and read all content completely anonymously.

Client-Side Only

AISimply is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It operates entirely on your device (client-side). We do not run any server-side code, databases, or tracking systems that would collect information about you.

Browser Functionality

Our website may use browser features like localStorage for:

  • Storing your theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Storing your language preference (English/French)

These preferences are stored locally on your device and never sent to any server.

3. Cookies

We do not use cookies. AISimply does not set or use cookies for tracking, analytics, advertising, or any other purpose. Your browsing activity is not tracked.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdSense (Potential)

We may implement Google AdSense for advertising in the future. If and when we do:

  • Google may collect anonymized data for ad targeting
  • You will be informed when this is active
  • You can use Google's ad personalization settings to control ad preferences
  • Your personal data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy

Google Analytics (Potential)

We may implement Google Analytics for aggregate site statistics in the future. If and when we do:

  • Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data (page views, session duration, device type)
  • No personal data like IP addresses or unique identifiers are stored
  • You can opt out using your browser's Do Not Track feature
  • You will be informed when this is active

Affiliate Links

We may include affiliate links to tools and services we recommend. When disclosing affiliate relationships:

  • We clearly mark affiliate links as such
  • Your decision to click an affiliate link is yours alone
  • We only recommend tools we genuinely believe are valuable
  • Affiliate commissions do not influence our recommendations

5. GDPR Compliance

Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request what personal data we hold about you (we hold none)
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate data (not applicable)
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (we don't store any)
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format (not applicable)
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data (not applicable)

Legal Basis

We do not process personal data, so GDPR's legal basis requirements do not apply.

6. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data to be compromised. Your privacy is protected by default through our architecture, not by encryption or firewalls.

7. Children's Privacy

AISimply is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from children or anyone else. Our content is designed for educational purposes and is appropriate for family viewing.

8. Contact & Data Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights under GDPR, please contact us through the information provided on our website.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of AISimply after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Third-Party Links

AISimply contains links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites. Please review their privacy policies before sharing your information.

Your Trust

Privacy is fundamental to our values. We built AISimply to be the most transparent and privacy-respecting AI education site available. We don't use dark patterns, hidden tracking, or tricks to collect data. Your trust is our most valuable asset, and we take it seriously.
