About AISimply

Our Mission

AISimply exists to make artificial intelligence and modern technology accessible to everyone. We believe that AI shouldn't be complicated, jargon-filled, or behind a paywall. Our mission is to demystify AI and technology in plain English (and French) so that anyone can understand and benefit from these transformative tools.

Who We Are

AISimply is created and maintained by The AISimply Team — a group of technology enthusiasts and educators dedicated to making complex concepts simple. We're not affiliated with any AI company, and we have no agenda other than helping people learn.

What We Offer

Our blog features over 50 articles covering:

  • AI Basics — Understanding how artificial intelligence, machine learning, and neural networks actually work
  • AI Tools — Discovering and comparing the best AI tools available today, from ChatGPT to image generators
  • How-To Guides — Step-by-step tutorials to help you get started with AI and automation
  • Tech Essentials — Explaining core technology concepts like cloud computing, APIs, cybersecurity, and blockchain

Our Principles

Free Forever: All content on AISimply is completely free. No paywall, no premium tiers, no hidden costs. Education about AI should be accessible to everyone.

No Tracking: We respect your privacy. AISimply collects zero personal data. We don't use cookies for tracking, we don't build profiles on you, and we don't sell your information. You can read our full privacy policy for details.

Bilingual Content: Every article is available in both English and French. We want to reach audiences across language barriers and make AI education truly inclusive.

Beginner-Friendly: We write for people new to AI and technology. No assumptions about prior knowledge. If we use technical terms, we explain them clearly. Complicated concepts are broken down into everyday language.

Honest and Independent: We're not selling anything. Our reviews and recommendations are honest. If we mention tools, we discuss their pros and cons fairly.

Why We Built This

AI is changing the world, but most people feel left behind by hype, jargon, and paywalled courses. We created AISimply because we believe everyone deserves to understand the technology shaping their future — without needing a computer science degree or a credit card.
