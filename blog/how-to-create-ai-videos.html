AI Script Writing

Every great video starts with a great script. AI can generate scripts tailored to your video's purpose.

Using ChatGPT for Scripts

Prompt Example:

Write a 3-minute video script for an explainer video about "How Machine Learning Works"
Target audience: High school students
Tone: Educational but engaging, avoid heavy jargon
Include: Hook, explanation, real-world example, call-to-action

Format: [SPEAKER NAME] [ACTION] [DIALOGUE]

Output format example:

[SPEAKER] [Looking at camera, friendly smile]
"Ever wondered how your Netflix recommendations work? Let's dive into machine learning."

[SPEAKER] [Gestures to screen]
"Machine learning is when computers learn from data patterns..."

Script Best Practices

  • Hook first (0-5 seconds): Grab attention immediately
  • Clear structure: Intro → Main points → Call-to-action
  • Conversational tone: Write like you're talking, not reading
  • Keep it concise: ~150 words per minute of video
  • Include visual cues: Describe what should appear on screen

Step-by-Step: Creating with HeyGen

Step 1: Sign Up and Choose an Avatar

Go to heygen.com, create an account (free tier available), and browse avatar options. HeyGen offers 100+ realistic avatars. Choose one that fits your brand.

Step 2: Upload or Write Your Script

Paste your video script. HeyGen shows character limit—typically 1,000-2,000 characters per video.

Step 3: Select Voice and Language

HeyGen uses AI text-to-speech. Choose from dozens of voices in multiple languages. You can also upload your own voiceover if preferred.

Step 4: Customize Avatar

Adjust:

  • Background (solid color, gradient, custom image)
  • Avatar position and size
  • Hand gestures and movements
  • Speaking pace and tone

Step 5: Preview and Render

Preview the video to check timing, sync, and overall feel. Make adjustments. Once satisfied, render to HD video.

Step 6: Download and Post

Download the MP4 file. Share on YouTube, social media, or embed on your website.

Pro Tip: Test different avatars and voices. A professional avatar with a matching voice creates more impact than mismatched choices. Spend 10 minutes auditioning options.

Text-to-Video Platforms

Text-to-video tools create video directly from text descriptions—no avatars needed. This is newer technology with amazing potential.

Available Platforms

  • Runway Gen-2: Generates video from detailed descriptions ($25/mo, limited free tier)
  • Pika: Simple text-to-video creator (free for short videos)
  • Descript: AI editing + generation ($24/mo)

How It Works

You describe what you want to see: "A serene beach at sunset with waves crashing gently, birds flying overhead, warm orange light reflecting on water."

The AI generates a short video (typically 4-16 seconds) matching your description.

Use Cases

  • B-roll for marketing videos
  • Visual explanations of concepts
  • Product demonstrations
  • Short social media content

AI Video Editing

Editing traditionally takes hours. AI accelerates this process significantly.

AI Editing Capabilities

  • Auto-cut silences: Remove pauses and dead air
  • Captions: Generate and sync automatically
  • B-roll matching: Insert matching visuals at mentions
  • Highlight detection: Find and enhance important moments
  • Background removal: Clean up distracting backgrounds

AI Editing Tools

  • Descript: Edit by transcription, AI removes filler words ($24/mo)
  • Opus Clip: Auto-shorts generator for TikTok/YouTube Shorts (free/paid)
  • Adobe Premiere Pro: AI features like auto-captions (included with subscription)
  • CapCut: Free AI editing for mobile and desktop

AI Voiceover Generation

Natural-sounding voiceovers without hiring voice actors.

AI Text-to-Speech Quality

Modern AI voices sound remarkably natural. Providers like Google Cloud TTS, Azure Speech, and ElevenLabs offer dozens of voices with different accents and tones.

Tools with Good Voices

  • ElevenLabs: Most natural-sounding voices ($5+/mo)
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech: Many languages, good quality (pay-per-use)
  • HeyGen built-in: Included with avatar generation
  • Descript Overdub: Clone your own voice ($24/mo)

Voice Selection Tips

  • Match voice to content (professional for training, casual for YouTube)
  • Test multiple voices before committing to a video
  • Adjust speech rate for clarity (slightly slower is better)
  • Use female/male voice to match avatar gender

Music & Sound Design

Audio is 50% of the viewing experience. Use the right music and sound effects.

Royalty-Free Music Sources

  • Epidemic Sound: Unlimited music ($10/mo)
  • Artlist: Curated music library ($20+/mo)
  • Freepik/Pixabay: Free stock music
  • YouTube Audio Library: Free for YouTube creators

Sound Design Best Practices

  • Choose music that matches video tone (upbeat for marketing, calm for training)
  • Keep music volume lower than voiceover
  • Use sound effects for transitions and emphasis
  • Avoid overly complex or distracting music

Complete Video Workflow

Timeline for a 5-Minute Video:

  • Planning (10 min): Decide topic, format, style
  • Scripting (20 min): Write or use AI to generate script
  • Avatar/Voice setup (10 min): Choose avatar, voiceover style
  • Video generation (30 min): Create in HeyGen or similar
  • Editing (20 min): Add captions, music, effects
  • Review and render (15 min): Final checks, export HD
  • Total: ~105 minutes (1.75 hours)

Workflow Steps

  1. Ideation: What story are you telling? Who's your audience?
  2. Script: Write or generate with ChatGPT
  3. Visuals: Choose avatar + background OR describe scene for text-to-video
  4. Voice: Select AI voice or upload your own
  5. Generate: Create video in HeyGen/Synthesia/Runway
  6. Enhance: Add captions, music, sound effects in Descript or CapCut
  7. Export: Render in HD, optimize for platform (YouTube, TikTok, etc.)
  8. Publish: Upload with SEO-friendly title and description

Advanced Tip: Repurposing Content

One long video can become multiple short videos:

  • Use Opus Clip to auto-generate YouTube Shorts
  • Extract key quotes as TikTok clips
  • Turn transcript into blog posts
  • Create social media snippets from key frames
Bottom Line: AI video creation is now accessible to anyone. With a good script and minimal technical skills, you can produce professional-quality videos in under 2 hours. The technology is advancing rapidly—newer tools like Sora and text-to-video will make this even easier. Start with HeyGen or Synthesia, refine your process, and scale. Video is the future of content—AI makes it achievable at any budget.
